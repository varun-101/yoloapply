Voice Interview — browser-native demo

100% in-browser (Web Speech API). No server, no API keys, no LLM. The interviewer is a small scripted loop so you can feel the voice in/out, barge-in, and pacing before we wire up the real thing.

Your browser doesn't expose SpeechRecognition. This demo needs Chrome or Edge (desktop), served over localhost or HTTPS. Speech synthesis (the interviewer's voice) may still work below.
Idle — press Start to begin.
The interview transcript will appear here. The interviewer speaks, then listens for your answer. Pause for ~1.5s and it'll take your turn, then cross-question or move on.

Tips: Start kicks off the first question. After the interviewer finishes talking it auto-listens — just speak. Barge-in works: start talking while it speaks and it'll stop. If auto-listen is flaky on your machine, use Hold to answer to push-to-talk.
